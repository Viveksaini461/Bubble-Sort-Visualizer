About PlayZone

PlayZone is a lightweight, privacy-conscious site offering free browser games that run on any device.

Our mission

Deliver delightful, fast, and fair games that anyone can play in seconds—no logins, no installs.

What you can play

  • Tic Tac Toe with AI or local 2-player
  • Snake with adjustable speed
  • Rock Paper Scissors with animations
  • Memory Card Match with a countdown timer
  • Typing Speed Test with WPM & accuracy

Privacy first

We store only essential data in your browser via localStorage to track scores and leaderboards.
