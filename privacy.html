Introduction

PlayZone values your privacy. This policy describes what we collect and how we use it.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal data on our servers. Game scores and preferences (like theme) are stored locally in your browser via localStorage.

Cookies

We may use cookies in the future for analytics or ads. If Google AdSense is enabled, it may use cookies to personalize ads. You can opt out of personalized ads in your Google account.

Third-party services

Links to third-party websites are provided for convenience. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Your choices

  • Switch between light and dark themes at any time.
  • Clear your browser storage to remove local game data.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us via the Contact page.
